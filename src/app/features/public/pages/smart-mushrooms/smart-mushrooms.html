What is the Smart Mushroom Pilot?

A complete pathway for farmers, groups and aspiring growers.

Smart Mushroom farming combines a clean, insulated growing room, environmental sensors, simple automation and a farmer dashboard. The Kenya pilot at JKUAT demonstrates how these pieces work together and trains farmers to use them confidently.

You can build with guidance, ask the JKUAT Smart Mushroom Team to build the room, or begin with training and grow into a complete setup.

Part 1 · Build your mushroom house

Clean, insulated and easy to control.

You can build the room yourself using our guides, or ask the JKUAT Smart Mushroom Team to construct it to the required growing-room specifications.

Option A · Build it yourself
  1. Pour a solid concrete base to keep pests out and level the room.
  2. Build a sturdy metal frame to support the walls and roof.
  3. Use pumice blocks or a pumice-concrete mix for thick, sealed walls. A starting mix is 4 parts pumice to 1 part cement; confirm the final design with a qualified builder.
  4. Add reflective insulation, shade netting and a waterproof roof with an overhang.
Option B · Let the team build it

Our JKUAT Smart Mushroom Team can build the complete room, including shelves, ventilation, humidification, lighting and safe sensor access.

Part 2 · Install the smart sensors

The room gets a set of reliable eyes.

The JKUAT team handles placement, wiring and configuration. Farmers do not need to programme the equipment.

DHT22

Temperature and humidity

Three sensors at different shelf heights reveal how evenly the room is performing.

SCD30

Carbon dioxide

One sensor sits in the centre of the room, opposite the racks, to guide fresh-air exchange.

BH1750

Light

One sensor measures light near the upper wall without direct LED glare.

Water level

Protect the humidifier

A water-level sensor prevents the humidifier and pump from running dry.

Who installs them?The JKUAT Smart Mushroom Team installs and tests the sensors, connects the ESP32 and Raspberry Pi, and confirms the room is ready for dashboard registration.

Suggested smart-farm hardware list

  • ESP32 development board
  • Raspberry Pi 4
  • DHT22 temperature and humidity sensors
  • CO₂ sensor
  • Light sensor
  • Water level sensor
  • Soil moisture sensor (optional)
  • Exhaust fan, humidifier, heater, LED grow lights and water pump
  • Relay module, LCD display, power supply and jumper wires

Part 3 · Connect to the farmer dashboard

Your farm, visible from wherever you are.

The sensors send room information to a Farmer's Online Dashboard, also called the directorate. Use a phone or computer to check conditions and respond early.

  1. Sign up. Create your farmer or organisation account.
  2. Register your farm. Add the farm name, location and room details.
  3. Connect your room. The JKUAT team links the sensors to your profile.
  4. Monitor remotely. View temperature, humidity, CO₂, equipment status and alerts.
