Why It Matters

Transforming agriculture with AI

BRIDGE-AI Kenya addresses three critical areas for sustainable development.

Farm Systems

Climate-smart mushroom farming

IoT sensors, GenAI analytics and digital shadows optimise growing conditions, reduce waste and increase yields for Kenyan mushroom farmers.

People & Skills

Inclusive digital skills

Hands-on training in AI, IoT and digital agriculture empowers youth, women and smallholder farmers building capacity for the future.

Regional Capacity

African GenAI capacity

Local expertise in generative AI for agriculture creates a replicable model that scales across East Africa and beyond.

JKUAT's Role

What JKUAT is doing

Leading the Smart Mushroom pilot and WP5 capacity building in Kenya.

PILOT

Smart Mushroom Pilot

GenAI-assisted monitoring, anomaly detection, digital shadows, IoT sensing and farmer advisory tools for mushroom grow rooms at JKUAT Smart Farm Zone.

LAB

Kenya Living Lab

Co-design and feedback activities with farmers, researchers, youth, women, advisors and SMEs to ensure solutions meet local needs.

TRAIN

Training & Bootcamps

Hands-on training programs, bootcamps and SME mentoring build digital skills and enable replication across East Africa.

MENTOR

SME Mentoring

Supporting agricultural SMEs to adopt, adapt and scale smart farming solutions through mentoring and technical assistance.

SCALE

Replication Toolkit

Playbooks, training materials and open repositories enable replication of the Smart Mushroom model across East Africa.

WP5

WP5 Leadership

Leading Work Package 5: Capacity Building and Replication, building African expertise in GenAI solutions for agriculture.
