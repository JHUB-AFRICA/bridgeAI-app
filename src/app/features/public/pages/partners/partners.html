Consortium

A strong Europe-Africa partnership

The BRIDGE-AI consortium brings together a multidisciplinary team of partners from Europe and Africa, combining complementary expertise in artificial intelligence, agriculture, Earth observation, digital technologies and innovation ecosystems.

Coordinated by Eurecat, the consortium includes research centres, universities and technology providers from Spain, Kenya, Tunisia, Nigeria and Austria. Together, they cover the full value chain of the project, from data collection and AI development to real-world validation, capacity building and knowledge transfer.

This collaborative approach ensures that advanced European technologies are effectively combined with local knowledge and field experience, enabling the development of solutions that are both technically robust and relevant to real agricultural contexts in Africa.
